# Questions a judge should ask

**Why not Fresha waitlist?** Notifications already exist. This demonstration links one cancellation’s price calculation, eligible external audience, policy-bound execution, observation, revised plan and outcome in one visible record. We do not claim Fresha lacks automation or AI.

**Why not ClassPass?** ClassPass already describes demand protection and price/conversion/expected-revenue optimization. The proposed wedge is merchant-controlled cancellation orchestration and action-level accountability. Yield management and discovery alone are not novel.

**Why not a discount marketplace?** The workflow may preserve full price, wait or require review. The marketplace is an execution surface; monitoring, revised strategy and attribution are the experiment.

**Why AI?** Arithmetic, ranking and policy do not need it. Context interpretation and adapting an allowlisted plan may benefit from a language model. That benefit requires empirical validation; a numerical policy benchmark does not prove it.

**Why an agent?** One goal persists through observation, repricing, reach changes, publishing and booking. A chatbot answer would not update the calendar or revenue state. The server-owned demo clock now runs from persisted, leased jobs and continues after all browser pages close on a standalone backend. Serverless requires a real external scheduler plus PostgreSQL.

**Why Nebius/NVIDIA?** A successful live planner will use an available NVIDIA Nemotron through Token Factory, with catalogue discovery and validated JSON. This is the actual integration boundary, not a badge. Until credentials are connected, neither latency nor model superiority is claimed.

**When the agent is wrong?** Unsupported/invalid plans, failed inference or policy gates require review. It cannot bypass money constraints. An allowed but wrong strategy can still lose revenue; logs and outcome measurement are necessary.

**How prevent over-discounting?** Floor, cap, margin, natural-demand protection and expected-revenue scoring constrain it. They do not eliminate cannibalization: the final synthetic unnecessary-discount result is poor. A causal merchant pilot is required.

**Merchant control?** Autopilot, repricing, price/margin/radius, support and approval limits; exact-revision approval.

**Recovered revenue?** A confirmed sandbox booking counted once. Recovery fraction is booked gross dollars / cancelled original value. This is not incremental lift or profit. Evaluation also counts naturally filled slots explicitly.

**Production data?** Merchant prices, cost/margin, capacity, cancellations/lead time, offered prices/exposure, responses, direct-fill baseline, opt-in preferences and booking reliability. Credible counterfactuals are needed for causal price elasticity. Sensitive attributes are unnecessary.

**Business model?** Recovery-linked performance fee, possibly plus SaaS, remains a hypothesis. No unsupported percentage or willingness to pay is invented. Acquisition commissions are not cancellation-only success fees.
