## Judging and interpretation

Four equally weighted criteria: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Quality of the Idea. [Rules §6](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules).

Our implementation response: prove real inference; ship one complete merchant-to-consumer transaction; quantify synthetic results honestly; explain the closed recovery loop. These are project choices, not additional official rules.

The [latest judge workshop update](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/updates/47163-recording-will-your-project-ship) prioritizes a real audience and an early aha moment. Do not substitute a sponsor badge for runtime evidence.
